## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, editorial atmosphere for communal travel and shared treasury escrow in India. The experience prioritizes serenity over transactional urgency, presenting shared group expenses with the poise of an independent quarterly travel journal rather than an aggressive fintech utility.

### Emotional Disposition & Tone
The design evokes ease, quiet luxury, absolute trust, and understated fellowship. It strips away the anxiety of split bills, group calculations, and currency exchanges, re-framing them as collaborative milestones within a grander narrative. Whitespace is generous, unhurried, and deliberate, treating candid travel imagery and itinerary fragments like archival prints on heavy bond paper.

### Design Movement
**Editorial Minimalism infused with Tactile Warmth.**
- Pure white gives way to an organic, tactile paper surface that mimics warm off-white newsprint and heavy art stock.
- The interface relies on hairline divisions, generous margins, and disciplined typographic rhythm rather than heavy drop shadows or decorative gradients.
- Financial figures are treated as factual records, using clear Indian number formatting (lakhs and crores) and avoiding garish status signifiers.

## Colors

The palette is derived from coastal Indian topography: shaded palms, calm brackish lagoons, raw linen paper, and sun-struck marigold garlands.

### Core Palette Roles
- **Paper (`#F7F8F6`):** The primary canvas across all views. Soft, light-absorbent, and soothing under natural or evening light. Never substitute with `#FFFFFF` except within pure high-key photo cutouts.
- **Ink (`#10201C`):** Deep botanical charcoal replacing harsh digital black. Used for primary narrative titles, metadata, and active text.
- **Lagoon (`#1E6F64`):** Primary action and calm validation. Signifies successful escrow contributions, confirmed reservations, and primary actions. It functions as an indicator of harmony rather than aggressive validation.
- **Marigold (`#F0A92B`):** Warm golden accent reserved for moments of celebration: completed collections, final bookings, special group milestones, and trip launch triggers. Never used as a generic warning signifier.
- **Frost (`#E6E4FA`):** Soft, muted lavender designated for pending votes, held funds, frozen escrow vaults, or disputed ledger items.
- **Mist (`#DDE3E0`):** Hairline borders, dividers, subtle structural grids, and unselected state tracks.
- **Sand (`#EFEFEA`):** Neutral subtle fill used for recessed cards, photo backing placeholders, and inactive badge states.

### Financial Polarity Guidelines
Do not use standard accounting red/green indicator colors to denote balances owed or received. Never render amounts in crimson red or electric emerald. Financial balance relationships must be expressed purely through concise editorial phrasing ("You have paid ₹45,000", "Balance settled"), typographic hierarchy, and the deep, tranquil presence of Lagoon or Ink.

## Typography

The type system blends the classical presence of **Playfair Display** with the clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Headlines & Narrative Moments (Playfair Display):** Used for trip destinations, invitations, day-by-day journal headers, and large milestone prompts. Avoid all-caps settings; italic variants are welcomed for poetic sub-headers or dates (e.g., *October in Fort Kochi*).
- **Interface & Mechanics (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs buttons, ledger details, settings, and body descriptions. Maintains high legibility even at reduced mobile viewports.
- **Numerical Ledger Figures & Escrow Vaults:** All financial figures must use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned columns in split sheets. Indian currency syntax (₹1,24,500 rather than ₹124,500) is standard across all tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout leans on generous spacing that lets editorial photography and ledger entries breathe naturally.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout bounded by `margin` (1.25rem / 20px) and `space-md` gaps. Full-bleed edge-to-edge photo components override outer margin constraints.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid with `gutter-tablet` (1.5rem / 24px) and `margin-tablet` (2rem / 32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at 1280px width, centered with `margin-desktop` (4rem / 64px) to preserve an intimate magazine spread aesthetic rather than a stretched dashboard.

### Spatial Rhythm
Dense stacks of rows are forbidden. Ledger items and itinerary stops have at least `space-md` between units, separating thematic blocks with `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows and plastic skeuomorphism, relying instead on tonal layering, delicate structural hairlines, and photographic depth.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Canvas:** The foundational background is `#F7F8F6` (Paper).
- **Cards and Enclosures:** Raised elements use either pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or a soft background shift to `#EFEFEA` (Sand), edged by a 1px border of `#DDE3E0` (Mist).
- **Shadow Signature:** When elevation is required for floating sheets, drawers, or toolbars, employ an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow tinted with the primary hue: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(16, 32, 28, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 32, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Photo Scrims:** Editorial imagery featuring text overlays requires a fine directional gradient scrim running from `rgba(16, 32, 28, 0.40)` at the edge fading smoothly to `rgba(16, 32, 28, 0.0)` over 60% of the image span.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is soft and tailored, avoiding both harsh right angles and juvenile hyper-rounded bubbles.

### Geometry Standards
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) establishes an architectural containment for photos, itinerary schedules, and split summaries.
- **Interactive Controls & Fields:** 12px corner radius for primary buttons, form inputs, and select triggers.
- **Badges, Chips & Meta Indicators:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for participant tags, approval chips, and category tags.
- **Hairlines:** Thin borders always maintain a 1px thickness; line-based icons must maintain consistent 1.25px to 1.5px stroke weight with rounded caps.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Lagoon Button):** Background `#1E6F64`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 12px. Padding 14px 24px. Height 48px. State transitions are deliberate and smooth (200ms ease-out). No harsh inset bevels.
- **Secondary Action (Subtle Paper):** Background `#EFEFEA` or transparent with a 1px `#DDE3E0` border, text `#10201C`.
- **Milestone CTA (Marigold Accent):** Background `#F0A92B`, text `#10201C` with `font-weight: 600`. Applied exclusively to collective moments: "Lock Itinerary", "Release Vault Escrow".

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- **Editorial Travel Card:** Backed in `#FFFFFF`, bordered in 1px `#DDE3E0`, 16px border-radius. Internal padding of 20px. When paired with cover imagery, the media is inset by 8px or full-bleed across top boundaries with matching 16px top corner masks.
- **Vault Status Card:** Recessed `#EFEFEA` container housing locked group funds. Shows a clear tabular balance (`currency-display`), participant avatar rings, and a Frost (`#E6E4FA`) pill indicator if locked in escrow pending verification.

### Ledger & Split List Items
- **Expense Row:** Hairline border bottom (`#DDE3E0`, 1px). Padding 16px 0. Avatar of payer on the far left (36px circle), title in `title-md`, and date/category in `label-sm` (`#10201C` at 60% opacity).
- **Settlement Status:** Financial splits display right-aligned using tabular numbers (`₹4,850`). Avoid red/green badge treatments; use `label-md` with concise wording ("You contributed", "Settled by Aarav") in `#1E6F64` or `#10201C`.

### Form Controls & Text Inputs
- **Inputs:** Height 52px, border-radius 12px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DDE3E0`. Focus state replaces border with `#1E6F64` and adds a subtle 1px matching ring.
- **Currency Entry Field:** Minimalist design with no enclosing border; display text size (`currency-display`), prefixed by the Indian Rupee symbol (₹) in a slightly lighter weight.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Chips:** Height 32px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with 14px horizontally. Default state: `#EFEFEA` fill, `#10201C` text. Selected state: `#1E6F64` fill, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Imagery & Scrims
- Full bleed or framed by generous paper borders.
- Scrims on photo cards must preserve the natural richness of the travel photo while ensuring titles in pure white or off-white are legible.
